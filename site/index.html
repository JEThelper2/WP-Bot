How It Works

Three steps from "never heard of this" to "my website is updated."

1

Connect your WordPress site

Send "set up my website" to our WhatsApp number. We'll walk you through creating a WordPress application password — a safe, limited-access credential that can't change your site settings.

2

Text what you want to change

Type a message describing what you need — just like you'd tell a staff member. "Post a job", "Update our hours", "Add an announcement about the holiday" — we understand plain English.

3

Confirm and it's live

We'll show you exactly what will be published and ask for a quick YES. That's it — your site is updated. Made a mistake? Reply UNDO within 24 hours to reverse it instantly.

Your Site, Your Rules

We know handing over site access feels risky. Here's what we do to keep you safe.

✓

Confirm before every publish

Nothing goes live without your explicit YES. You see exactly what will be published before it happens.

✓

Undo within 24 hours

Made a mistake? Reply UNDO and it's reversed — the change is fully reverted, not just hidden.

✓

Editor access only — never Admin

We use a dedicated WordPress user with limited Editor permissions. We can't change your settings, install plugins, or touch your users.

✓

No permanent deletes

When we remove a post, it goes to WordPress Trash — fully recoverable from your WordPress admin if needed.

✓

Passwords encrypted, never logged

Your application password is encrypted at rest and never appears in any log or error message.
