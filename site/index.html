Your site, your rules.

We know handing over site access feels risky. Here's what we do to keep you safe.

rule

Nothing publishes without your approval

Every change shows you exactly what will be published and asks for your explicit YES before anything goes live.

undo

Made a mistake? Undo it.

Reply UNDO within 24 hours and the change is fully reverted. Posts go to Trash, not permanent deletion. You're always in control.

admin_panel_settings

Limited access only — we can't break your site

The assistant only has permission to manage posts and media. It cannot change your site settings, install plugins, or modify users.
