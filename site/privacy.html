Privacy Policy

We believe in Intelligent Simplicity. That means your data should be handled securely, transparently, and with strict minimal access. This document outlines how we protect your information.

lock

The "Limited Access" Principle

Our core philosophy is simple: we only access what we absolutely need to make your experience seamless. Sitepaw operates on a strict "Limited Access" framework, ensuring that your business data remains exactly that—yours.

We do not read your general WhatsApp conversations. We only process commands explicitly directed to the assistant.

encrypted

Data Encryption & Handling

WhatsApp Data

Messages sent to Sitepaw are transmitted securely using industry-standard encryption. We transiently process the text to execute your WordPress commands and immediately discard the raw message data from our active memory once the action is confirmed.

WordPress Access Tokens

Your WordPress API credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. They are never displayed in plain text within our dashboard or logs. We use these tokens strictly to authenticate requests you initiate via WhatsApp.

Business Information

Basic profile information required to maintain your account (such as your phone number and email address) is stored securely. We never sell, rent, or share your business metrics or contact information with third-party advertisers.

verified_user

Your Rights

You have complete control over your connection. You can revoke WordPress access tokens directly from your WordPress admin panel at any time, instantly severing Sitepaw's ability to interact with your site. Furthermore, you may request complete deletion of your account and associated encrypted credentials by contacting support.
